Raspberry Pi Drive Sync

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Raspberry Pi Drive Sync is a private application operated for personal use by its owner. This policy explains how the application accesses and handles Google user data.

1. Information accessed

The application may access files and associated metadata in the owner's Google Drive account, within the permissions granted during Google OAuth authorization.

These files may include personal backups, Garmin activity reports, and health-data exports.

2. How information is used

Google Drive access is used exclusively to upload, download, organize, and synchronize the owner's personal files between Google Drive and a Raspberry Pi computer.

The application does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

3. Storage and security

Files are stored on the owner's Raspberry Pi and in the owner's Google Drive account. OAuth authentication credentials are stored locally in the rclone configuration.

Access to the Raspberry Pi and its credentials is controlled by the owner. Communication with Google Drive uses Google's authenticated API.

4. Data sharing

The application does not intentionally sell, disclose, or transfer Google user data to advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

Google processes information as necessary to operate Google Drive and its API.

5. Data retention and deletion

Files remain in Google Drive or on the Raspberry Pi until the owner deletes them or changes the synchronization configuration.

The owner can revoke the application's Google account permissions at any time using Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously synchronized files.

6. Application users

This application is intended solely for the account owner's personal use. It does not offer public registration or accounts for other users.
